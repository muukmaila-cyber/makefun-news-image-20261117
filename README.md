# Tuesday 17 November 2026 — The World in One Page

A newspaper front page that sums up Tuesday 17 November 2026's world
news in a single image. Entry for the make.fun bounty
**news-image-20261117** ("Sum up 17 November's world news in one image").

## What the image shows

45 verified stories from Tuesday 17 November 2026, drawn as a front
page on cream paper:

- **Masthead:** "Tuesday 17 November 2026" — 45 stories, 6 continents,
  all from Tuesday.
- **Three lead stories:** the Crimean Bridge hit again (a 227 m span
  collapsed, 2 killed); the US–Iran war on day 264 (IRGC drones at a US
  radar in Bahrain intercepted, US jets strike Qeshm Island, two tankers
  hit); the Quad summit in Manila (Trump, Modi, Albanese, Takaichi).
- **Ten briefs:** Nvidia's record $115.84bn quarter, Ebola passing
  11,000 cases in DR Congo, COP31 ministers taking over in Antalya,
  Mongolia's president resigning, Serbia's student-led list sworn in,
  the Russian An-12 crash (19 killed), Mondlane's trial in Mozambique,
  the US, Mexico and Bolivia losing measles-free status, Gaza's winter
  crisis, and Japan scrambling jets at Russian planes.
- **A world map** ("Where it happened") with numbered pins, plus the
  numbered list "Also on Tuesday 17 November" (stories 14–45): Turkey
  seizing Sözcü TV, the Knesset picking Kahana 66–52, the West Bank
  shooting, the Bangladesh garment strike, Cyclone Akvan, the Banda Sea
  quake, the Prague train collision, the France strike, Tusk surviving
  in Poland, the EU–Rwanda asylum hub warning, the Apple outage,
  Ukraine's civilian deaths, Moldova's airspace closure, China–Canada
  tariff relief, the IMF's South Korea upgrade, Norway beating Denmark
  3–2, India beating Pakistan 2–1 in the SAFF final, Lisa Cook refusing
  the White House hearing, Deutsche Bank's fall, Pope Leo XIV's return,
  the FAA briefing, the NSA network outage, the Uttarakhand bus crash,
  Togo beating Algeria 3–0, the Pentagon warning, Araghchi in Oman,
  the Malaysia–France partnership, Thailand's tariff announcement, WFP
  aid in Kenya, the Schumer–Van Hollen fight, Australia's visa easing,
  and the Thomas Markle complaints.
- **Footer:** every story here happened or was first reported on
  Tuesday 17 November 2026. Contested claims are marked as claims.

Every fact on the page was verified against reporting from Reuters, AP,
AFP, BBC, Al Jazeera, WHO and the UN before drawing. Stories first
reported on another day were deliberately left out.

## How it was made

The image is drawn programmatically with Python and Pillow: paper,
rules, pictograms and the stylised world map are all code, so every
word on the page is exactly as written — nothing was generated by an
image model. The drawing script lives outside this repository; the
committed `entry.png` is its output.

## Running it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
Standard library only — no dependencies, no network access. Two runs
write byte-identical files.
